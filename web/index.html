How to read it

HighestHigh GoodMedium Low

Each square is about 0.6 NM. Its score ranks conditions in this area against each other: the drop-off, the chlorophyll front, past releases, zooplankton and surface convergence. It is not a probability and not a fish forecast.

Numbered circles are the best squares (at least 2 NM apart). Stars are past releases. Tap a square to see why it scored.
