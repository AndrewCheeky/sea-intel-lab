    How to read it

    HighestHigh GoodMedium Low

    Each square is about half a mile across. The darker the square, the more of the things marlin like come together there: the drop-off, the line where green water meets blue, temperature breaks and cooler water edges, bait, current lines, and recent catches. It's a guide to where to start looking, not a promise of fish.

    Numbered circles are the best spots in each area. Stars are recent releases. Tap a square to see what it has going for it.
